01what your team receives

One organisation, one sign-in, one endpoint.

Your own page

Your organisation signs in to its own page: your API key (copied there, never sent by email), your session prefix, your limits and your access window.

One HTTPS endpoint

One JSON command per call, from any agent, framework or language. Worked examples are on your page.

The exact instance

The engine build, the browser version and the host are declared on your page, so every result can be traced to what produced it.

Confidential by default

No organisation sees another's name, sessions or results.

02how access works

By invitation, after the NDA.

  1. I
    NDA

    Access begins once the NDA is countersigned.

  2. II
    Sign-in

    Your welcome note arrives by email; your platform password arrives separately.

  3. III
    Run

    Sign in, copy your API key and run your tasks within your window. Access can be paused, extended or ended on request.
